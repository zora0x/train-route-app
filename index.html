Board Anywhere

Works for any station pair in India. Every train on your journey is fetched with its full route — then seat availability is checked from each station on that route to your destination, including stations before where you board.
AVL n seats available RAC n against RAC WL n waitlist Regret closed % confirmation chance seats on 8 Nov that date opens up ● your stop earlier = boards before your station
How to use it: rows inside each train are every station that train stops at from its own origin through to your destination — including the ones before your station, marked earlier. A seat released at an earlier stop is still bookable for the leg you actually ride, and that is often the seat that is open when yours is not.
Boarding earlier costs more and adds waiting, so compare the fare column before you move.
Data: routes and station list from erail.in; live availability and confirmation predictions from ConfirmTkt. All fetched directly in your browser, no server and no API key.
Availability changes constantly — this reflects the moment you searched.
